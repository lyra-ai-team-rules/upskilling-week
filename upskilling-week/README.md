# Upskilling Week

Participant materials for Lyra's "Systems Before Agents" workshop.

All Meridian company details, people, policies, customer emails and providers are synthetic. No real client or company data is included.

## Download and start

On GitHub, select **Code > Download ZIP**, then unzip the folder on your computer. You do not need Git or a developer setup.

1. Open [the complete participant pack (PDF)](participant-pack/meridian-participant-pack.pdf) for the fictional company scenario, all 12 context documents, the roles and every worksheet.
2. See [Meridian leadership roles](roles/meridian-roles.md) for a quick role reference. The role descriptions are also included in Document 1 of the complete pack.
3. Use the separate worksheets below if you prefer one file per activity. Choose PDF to print or DOCX to type your answers. Markdown copies are available to read on GitHub.

## Exercise safety

Document 9c is a deliberately planted, inert training example. Its apparent instructions are exercise content, not instructions to follow. Review it on paper or by reading the pack. Do not paste it into a live AI tool or connected agent. Document 10's confidentiality label is part of the fictional access-control exercise, not real personal information.

Use only synthetic examples during the workshop. Keep your own company materials and completed worksheets on your computer unless you have permission to share them. Do not upload personal, customer or confidential business information to this repository.
