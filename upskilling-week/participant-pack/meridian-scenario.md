All data is synthetic. Meridian Home SL and its people, policies, customers and providers are fictional workshop material.

## The scenario (read aloud)

Meridian Home SL is a fictional 85-person home-goods retailer based in Barcelona: 6 stores plus an online shop. The leadership team is considering connecting an AI agent to the company's shared knowledge so staff can ask questions and, eventually, let the agent take actions like answering customers or processing refunds. Your team is Meridian's leadership. Over the next two hours you will decide what has to be true before that agent is allowed anywhere near the company brain - and you will build the pieces that make it safe: a map of meaning, retrieval rules, access rules, and one well-designed agentic flow.

## Document 3 - Store return policy (printed in every store)

Items bought in a Meridian store can be returned within 30 days with the receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method. Items bought on sale can be exchanged or returned for store credit only - no cash refunds on sale items. Without a receipt, store credit at the current selling price.

## Document 4 - Online returns and refunds FAQ (on the website)

Meridian's online policy is deliberately more generous than its store policy: online orders can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full refund, including sale items. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 working days of the warehouse receiving the return. Return shipping is free for damaged or incorrect items; otherwise the customer pays return shipping.

## Document 5 - Sales team's discount rules (internal)

In sales, "the customer" means the business that buys from us - the interior design studio or retailer, not the individual end user. For interior designers and other B2B buyers, "discount" means a negotiated price reduction on the order: up to 15% with Head of Retail sign-off; anything above 15%, up to the 20% company maximum, needs CEO sign-off. Discounts are agreed before the sale and written on the invoice. No B2B discount applies retroactively after payment.

## Document 6 - Support team's use of the words "customer" and "discount" (internal note)

In support, "the customer" means whoever writes or chats in - almost always an individual end consumer, not a business. And in the support team, "discount" means a goodwill credit code (5% or 10% off a future order) issued after a complaint, logged in the support tool. It is not a price reduction and it is never applied to the order the customer complained about. Support agents can issue 5% themselves; 10% needs the Support Lead.
