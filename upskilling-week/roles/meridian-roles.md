# Meridian leadership roles

All data is synthetic. Meridian Home SL and its people, policies, customers and providers are fictional workshop material.

Role descriptions from Document 1 of the participant pack. Use the company profile and context documents alongside these roles.

  - CEO - Mar Ortiz. Final sign-off on strategy and on any discount above 15% (20% is the company maximum). Owner, with the People Lead, of anything marked confidential.
  - Head of Retail - Pau Vidal. Runs the 6 stores and store staff. Approves B2B discounts up to 15%.
  - Head of Online - Sara Klose. Runs the online shop, online marketing and the web warehouse.
  - Support Lead - Nadia Ferre. Runs the 6-person support team (email and chat). Approves goodwill credits.
  - People Lead - Jordi Casas. Hiring, contracts, reorganizations. Owner, with the CEO, of all CONFIDENTIAL people documents.
  - Store managers - one per store, running each store day to day, reporting to the Head of Retail.
