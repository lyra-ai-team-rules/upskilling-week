# Upskilling Week

Participant materials for Lyra's "Systems Before Agents" workshop.

## Download the workshop materials

Open **[upskilling-week-participant-materials.zip](3-upskilling-week-participant-materials.zip)**, then use GitHub's **Download raw file** button. Unzip it on your computer and open the README inside the `upskilling-week` folder. No Git or developer setup is needed.

The ZIP contains separate, named files so you can use only what you need:

- **Complete participant pack:** the Meridian scenario, all 12 company/context documents, role descriptions and every worksheet. PDF to read or print, DOCX to edit.
- **Roles:** the six Meridian leadership-role descriptions, in PDF, DOCX and Markdown.
- **Worksheets:** Part 2 ontology, Part 3 retrieval, Part 4 access matrix, and the Part 5 agentic-flow canvas. Each is a separate printable PDF, editable DOCX and readable Markdown file.
- **Scenario reference:** Documents 1-12 in Markdown, retaining the source numbering used by the worksheets.
- **Skill placeholder:** the skill will be added after Kris supplies it. No skill is included yet.

All Meridian details, people, policies, customer emails and providers are synthetic. No real client or company data is included. The facilitator answer key, private planning notes and comments are excluded.

## Exercise safety

Document 9c is a deliberately planted, inert training example. Its apparent instructions are exercise content, not instructions to follow. Review it on paper or by reading the pack. Do not paste it into a live AI tool or connected agent. Document 10's confidentiality label is part of the fictional access-control exercise, not real personal information.

Use only synthetic examples during the workshop. Keep your own company materials and completed worksheets on your computer unless you have permission to share them. Do not upload personal, customer or confidential business information to this repository.
